Questions

Where do the robots come from?

From our factory at BotFactoryFX. It searches for trading strategies around the clock, judges the best with its Reality Check, and puts the ones that pass into quarantine. Some were found by StrategyQuant X, which the factory also runs; each file says who found it and how it was chosen. Each robot's full file, with its backtest and how it was found and judged, is on botfactoryfx.com.

Is the record real money?

No. In quarantine each robot runs on live market prices, from Darwinex's MetaTrader 5 (bitcoin, from Hyperliquid; the ones StrategyQuant X found, from Dukascopy, replayed by StrategyQuant X itself every Saturday), simulated with real costs and no real orders. That is the point of the quarantine: proof first, before anyone risks money on it.

Why can't I buy it now?

A robot goes on sale only once it is proven: 180 days in quarantine and better than 90 of 100 versions of itself with random entries. Until then you can follow it day by day and ask us to tell you when it is on sale.

Why can't a bigger bet make a bigger record?

Every trade is measured at the same risk: the size at which its own backtest would have fallen 20% at worst. Every robot on the board is measured the same way, so their records can be compared, and a backtest that hides its falls makes its robot fall sooner here.

Is a rising price a promise?

No. It tells you what the robot has done since it entered quarantine, not what it will do. Good records end too.

What do I get when I buy?

The robot compiled for your platform (each robot's page says which: MetaTrader 5, MetaTrader 4, cTrader, JForex…), the same one that runs in its quarantine, with a key tied to your real account (on a demo account it runs without one). No source code, except for a Python bot, which goes as code. Your order link always downloads its latest fixes, the ones that leave every trade as it was. Its public record keeps running.

What if its rules change?

Then it is a new robot: it enters quarantine at $100 with its own record, and the old one stays on the board, replaced.
