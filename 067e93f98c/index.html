How prices move

  1. 1

    Every robot starts at $100

    Whatever its backtest says, a robot comes onto the board at $100 the day it enters quarantine. Its backtest is shown next to it, but the price only listens to what it does from then on.

  2. 2

    Every trade goes on record

    In quarantine it runs on live market prices, from Darwinex's MetaTrader 5 (bitcoin, from Hyperliquid), simulated with real costs and no real orders. Each trade it closes goes on its record. No editing, no deleting. Losing trades stay.

  3. 3

    The price follows the record

    Every day at 09:00 Madrid time the price is reset: the listing price times what its record has grown since listing. If it loses, it gets cheaper.

Questions

Where do the robots come from?

From our factory at BotFactoryFX. It searches for trading strategies around the clock, judges the best with its Reality Check, and puts the ones that pass into quarantine. Each robot's full file, with its backtest and how it was found and judged, is on botfactoryfx.com.

Is the record real money?

No. In quarantine each robot runs on live market prices, from Darwinex's MetaTrader 5 (bitcoin, from Hyperliquid), simulated with real costs and no real orders. That is the point of the quarantine: proof first, before anyone risks money on it.

Why can't I buy it now?

A robot goes on sale only once it is proven: 180 days in quarantine and better than 90 of 100 versions of itself with random entries. Until then you can follow it day by day and ask us to tell you when it is on sale.

Why can't a bigger bet make a bigger record?

Every trade is measured at the same risk: the size at which its own backtest would have fallen 20% at worst. Every robot on the board is measured the same way, so their records can be compared, and a backtest that hides its falls makes its robot fall sooner here.

Is a rising price a promise?

No. It tells you what the robot has done since it entered quarantine, not what it will do. Good records end too.

What do I get when I buy?

The robot compiled for MetaTrader 5, the same one that runs in its quarantine, with a key tied to your real account (on a demo account it runs without one). No source code. Your order link always downloads its latest fixes, the ones that leave every trade as it was. Its public record keeps running.

What if its rules change?

Then it is a new robot: it enters quarantine at $100 with its own record, and the old one stays on the board, replaced.
